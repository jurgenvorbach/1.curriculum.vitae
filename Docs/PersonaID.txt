Persona ID verification
I noticed that people have lots of practical issues with Persona ID verification, but there are not many comments (and no media articles as far as I can tell) about the data privacy and security of this service run by a San Francisco based company, Persona Identities Inc., on behalf of LinkedIn. I personally aborted the registration when the service wanted access to the NFC of my passport and became even more concerned when I noticed the amount of data they collect on their privacy policy page (which I apparently can't link, so you will have to search for it).
